## Description (4000 chars max)

Echograph is a privacy-first voice recorder and transcriber. Audio is captured, transcribed, and summarized entirely on your iPhone — nothing is uploaded to any server.

POWERED BY WHISPER
At the core, Echograph runs OpenAI's Whisper speech-recognition model directly on Apple Neural Engine. State-of-the-art accuracy in 90+ languages, including English, Russian, German, French, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
Once a Whisper model is downloaded (the app prompts you on first use), Echograph works fully offline. Useful on planes, in courtrooms, in clinics, in interviews — anywhere reliable Wi-Fi isn't.

PROFESSIONAL FEATURES
• Word-level timestamps with tap-to-seek playback
• Search across every recording and transcript instantly
• Edit transcript text to correct names and rare terms
• Highlight key segments and filter by highlights
• Manual speaker labels with color coding
• Custom vocabulary biasing for legal, medical, and technical jargon
• Export to PDF, Word (.docx), Markdown, plain text, SRT, and VTT subtitles
• Send transcript snippets to Reminders or Calendar with one tap
• Translate any transcript using Apple's built-in Translation framework

APPLE INTELLIGENCE INSIDE (Pro+)
On iPhone 15 Pro and later running iOS 26, Echograph uses Apple's on-device language model to:
• Generate concise meeting summaries with action items
• Suggest topical tags automatically
• Answer questions about any recording — "what did the doctor say about the dose?"

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• Audio is processed entirely on-device. Period.
• No analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no data collection of any kind.
• Apple Privacy Label: "Data Not Collected".
• Open-source Whisper models. You can audit what runs.

PRICING
Free: Apple Speech transcription, full search, full export.
Pro: $19.99 one-time. Whisper Tiny / Base / Small / Large v3 Turbo, custom vocabulary, all formats.
Pro+: $29.99/year (or $4.99/mo, 7-day free trial). Apple Intelligence summaries, AI Q&A, auto-tagging.

WHO IT'S FOR
Journalists capturing interviews. Lawyers documenting client meetings. Doctors and therapists running private practices. Postgrad students sitting through 2-hour lectures. Podcasters drafting subtitles. Knowledge workers in privacy-conscious cultures.

WHY ECHOGRAPH
Most transcription apps upload your audio to a cloud server, charge per minute, and can change their privacy terms without notice. After the 2025 Otter.ai class action, that's not acceptable for sensitive recordings. Echograph runs the same Whisper model — but locally, on your phone, where it belongs.

Your voice. Your iPhone. Nothing else.
